# Can I connect my device?

A house number tells the postman where to go. But someone has to be home to open the door. If a device is fully off, its network card is off too, and the letter just lies on the doormat. So the device must be in a light sleep and understand one special kind of letter.

These are the most common kinds:

1. Wake-on-LAN: a standard wake-up letter sent to the device's MAC address. Many PCs and some TVs understand it once you turn it on in their settings.
2. The brand's own language: some devices only wake up for a letter with a secret code inside. You get that code by pairing the device once with an app.
3. Network standby: many smart TVs only listen when a setting like "Quick start" or "Wake on network" is on.
4. A smart plug: for simple devices like a lamp. You don't wake the device, you switch its power on.
5. The brand's cloud: this works too, but it needs an account and a key, and the letter travels over the internet.
Tip: The question is never "what is the address?". The question is "which letter does this device understand, and how do I get it?".
