# How does it work?

Think of your home network as a street. Your router is the postman, and every device in your house has its own house number: the last part of its IP address.

Every button in this app is a script in the Turn on folder. When you press a button, the app starts that script. The script writes a small letter and gives it to the postman, who brings it to the right house number. The device reads the letter and wakes up.

Every device understands its own kind of letter, so every device gets its own script. For example, the PS5 lives at number 42 and only wakes up for a letter with its own secret code inside.

Secret codes never leave your house and are never shown on screen.

You can also connect an AI agent: it runs the same scripts as the buttons. Two ways, one script.
