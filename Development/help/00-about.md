# About Creative Connection

Creative Connection is the bridge between you, your AI agent and your devices: all the power buttons of your home in one place, on your own computer. When you press a button in the app, a small script turns your device on. Your AI agent doesn't press any buttons. It simply runs that same script. Every button is a PowerShell script in the Turn on folder, so when you say "Hey AI, turn on my TV", your agent runs the TV script.

Why we made it: sometimes you want your AI agent to do something for you, but it can't. So we made our own. Instead of installing twenty different apps from twenty different brands, you get one simple app with a button for every device.

1. Everything is local. The app and its scripts only live on your own computer. Nothing is stored on a server or in the cloud.
2. The letters only travel inside your own home network. Your computer and your devices must be on the same network.
3. Anything that can run a PowerShell script on your computer, you or your AI agent, can use the same buttons.
4. You stay in charge. The app only does what you or your AI agent asks, and you always have the final say.
5. You may use it, change it and build on it, as long as you follow the Apache License 2.0 and keep the credits.
