# Read this first

Good to know! Some devices are easy to turn on, others are stubborn. If yours doesn't work right away, you may need to dig a little deeper: look at open source projects for your device, or find out how its official app talks to it. Only do this with your own devices.

This app turns on devices in your home with one button. Every button runs a small script.

Want your AI agent to set it up for you? Let it read every section in order, from top to bottom, before it does anything.

1. The first sections explain what the app is, how it works and how it looks.
2. The middle sections explain how to add a new device, step by step.
3. The last sections help with hard devices, problems and safety.
