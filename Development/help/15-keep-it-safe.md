# Keep it safe

1. Secret codes never go in a script, a log, the screen or a chat. A script reads them only at the moment it runs.
2. Letters only travel inside your home network, unless you choose the brand's cloud.
3. You always log in to your router yourself. The app and your AI agent never get your router password.
4. Every .ps1 file in the Turn on folder becomes a button. Never leave copies or backups there.
5. Test with the dry run first, then press the real button.
6. The human always has the final say. If you say no, your AI agent stops.
7. Your AI agent may run the scripts in the Turn on folder. It never adds or changes a script there until you have read it and said yes.
8. Read every script before you put it in the Turn on folder. A script can do anything you can do.
