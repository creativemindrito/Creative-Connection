# Give your device a house number

Before a device gets a button, it needs a house number that never changes. You set that in your router.

1. Make sure the device is on and connected to your home network, by cable or Wi-Fi.
2. Open your router's settings page in your browser. This is often 192.168.1.1 or 192.168.0.1.
Tip: The address and the password are usually printed on a sticker under your router.
3. Log in with your router password.
4. Look for the list of connected devices. It is often under Advanced settings, then DHCP or LAN. Every router names it a bit differently.
5. Find your device and look at its MAC address and its IP address. The last number of the IP address is its house number.
Tip: Some devices have two MAC addresses: one for Wi-Fi and one for the cable. Always pick the one the router is using right now.
6. Reserve that address for the device. This is often called "Add reservation", "Static lease" or "Fixed IP". Save your settings.
7. Now the device keeps the same house number, even after a restart.
